Making Unpaired Drifting Work for Single-Step Speech Enhancement

Audio demo — denoising on VoiceBank–DEMAND (16 kHz). Submitted to ICASSP 2027.

code repository

Our systems are trained without any paired data: the training set is the corpus of noisy mixtures plus a separate clean-speech bank (DNS-2020) with no correspondence between them, and enhancement is a single forward pass (no iterative sampler). The systems demoed here condition the drifting force on the input: anchors + key (β=1), anchors + gated key (a per-frame key strength driven by a CTC recognizer’s confidence, training-time only), and keyed β=1 + corrector (a second small network trained on the generator’s own renders of the clean corpus, raising quality without paired data).
